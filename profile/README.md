Peakbits & Co. builds digital twins of professional athletes, simulates the races around them and turns the results into decisions a team's staff can act on. Every number comes with its uncertainty and the sample behind it.

Everything runs on PerformKit, the modelling engines we build and own. Small language models, fine-tuned per task with MLX, run on Apple silicon the team owns and write briefs, debriefs and answers; every figure in them comes from an engine. [Derived](https://getderived.com), our iPhone app for athletes who train on their own, runs on the same engines.
