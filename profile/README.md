# Peakbits & Co.

**Simulation and decision models for professional sport.**

Peakbits & Co. builds digital twins of professional athletes, simulates the races and seasons
around them and turns the results into decisions a team's staff can act on. Every number comes
with its uncertainty and the sample behind it.

We are an independent studio for endurance analytics in road cycling, athletics and team
sports, based in Poland, EU. We take on a few teams and clubs each season, on engines we build
ourselves.

## What we work on

- **Road cycling.** Digital twins, race simulation and decision models for professional teams.
  Triathlon follows on the same foundations.
- **Athletics.** Distance running from 800 m to the marathon: critical speed, course projections
  and where a result leaves an athlete under the qualification rules.
- **Team sports clubs.** The endurance side of the game, from the tracking data a club already
  records: running profiles, peak demands by position, late-match fatigue and load across the
  fixture list.

## The work

| Model | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Digital twin** | Critical power, W′ and how each holds up as work accumulates, fitted to each rider's own efforts. Where the efforts cannot answer, the twin says so. |
| **Race simulation** | Course, wind, pacing, line-up and the other teams, sampled thousands of times. The answer is a distribution: what usually happens, what rarely does, and what it would take to change it. |
| **Decision models** | Selection and race programmes with hierarchical Bayesian models that pool each rider's data, add what the staff know and state where the evidence is thin. |
| **Validation** | Every projection is frozen before the start and scored after the finish. Calibration is measured, shown to the team and fed into the next model. |

We do not measure or optimise aerodynamics. We take the drag figures from a team's own aero
testing and compute what a change is worth on each course, in seconds and in watts.

## PerformKit

All of it runs on PerformKit, the modelling engines we build and own.

| Engine | Scope |
|---|---|
| **PerformTwin** | Physiological profile, race simulation and the scoring of every projection |
| **PerformCourse** | Courses, climbs and route geometry, from the GPX file to the segments the physics runs on |
| **PerformMetrics** | Training load, personal baselines and session metrics |
| **PerformAnalysis** | Correlation, change points, bootstrap intervals and false discovery control |

## The standard

Science first. Then software. Then AI. The same six rules hold for every team, club and athlete
we work with.

1. **Uncertainty on every number.** Every estimate arrives with its interval and the sample
   behind it.
2. **Refuse rather than approximate.** When the data cannot answer, we say so, and say what it
   would take.
3. **Physics where it is known.** Mechanistic models where the science is settled, machine
   learning where it is not.
4. **Corrected for multiple comparisons.** We control the false discovery rate across every
   family of tests and report what failed.
5. **Every projection scored.** We freeze every projection before the race and audit it after.
6. **Yours to keep.** A team's data, the models fitted to its athletes and every report are
   handed over in open formats, with no licence to renew. The engines stay ours.

## Private AI

Our language models run on a private cloud: Apple silicon dedicated to one team, reachable from
the service course, the team bus or a hotel room on race or match day.

- **One model per task.** Race briefs, draft debriefs and answers to staff questions each come
  from a small model fine-tuned for that task with MLX.
- **Engines compute, models explain.** A language model never produces a number. Every figure it
  cites comes from a PerformKit engine and links back to it.
- **Trained where the data lives.** Athlete data is never sent to a public AI service and never
  trains anyone else's model.
